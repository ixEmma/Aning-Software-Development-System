# Aning design architecture

Derived from Emmanuel's "Reusable Design Taste & UI Language" system.

## Core principle

Preserve **design judgment**, not one house style.

Different products should look different when their purpose, audience, environment, and emotional target differ. Consistency means shared decision quality, not repeated colors, radii, layouts, or decorative treatments.

## Decision order

Before visual styling:

1. Define product purpose, audience, environment, and primary workflows.
2. Define the emotional target.
3. Select the structural DNA: hierarchy, composition, density, pacing, focal behavior, and layout rhythm.
4. Select the personality layer.
5. Choose one primary design archetype and only useful supporting references.
6. Derive color from the personality and product purpose.
7. Choose typography, imagery, surfaces, controls, geometry, and motion that reinforce that direction.
8. Check anti-patterns and product constraints.
9. Explain intentional departures.

## Quality bar

The target is **intentional, art-directed composition**, not merely "modern", "minimal", or "premium".

Principles:
- every major scale, gap, crop, alignment, and grouping should earn its place;
- visual balance matters more than mathematical symmetry;
- the interface supports the product instead of constantly showing off;
- each section/screen should move the experience forward;
- visual weight determines what the eye sees first;
- brand personality should be visible before copy explains it;
- adapt the same quality bar differently across domains;
- prefer one dominant purpose/focal point per screen or section;
- control information density rather than displaying everything at once.

## Structural DNA

Structural DNA is the transferable logic underneath the styling:
- hierarchy;
- section sequencing;
- focal behavior;
- rhythm;
- spacing;
- balance;
- grouping;
- layout relationships.

Do not force every section into:
`heading -> paragraph -> cards -> CTA`.

Use:
- independent headings when pause/scale helps;
- offset or cropped visuals when hierarchy benefits;
- negative space intentionally;
- unequal modules when their jobs differ;
- one visual anchor with supporting elements;
- alternating dense/sparse moments;
- text-led and visual-led sections;
- different narrative jobs across the page.

A section may establish, explain, prove, demonstrate, compare, reassure, convert, or close. Repetition is not progression.

## Confidence discipline

Label reference-derived guidance as:
- **Observed** - directly visible;
- **Inferred** - likely from evidence but not confirmed;
- **Verified** - confirmed through implementation/runtime/source evidence.

A screenshot can verify composition. It cannot verify exact animation timing or interaction behavior.

## Required design brief

For a new product, produce:
- product intent;
- audience;
- emotional target;
- structural DNA;
- primary/supporting archetypes;
- palette direction and rationale;
- typography direction;
- imagery direction;
- component geometry;
- density;
- motion character;
- anti-patterns;
- references/evidence used.

Do not copy a reference literally. Extract its logic and adapt it.
